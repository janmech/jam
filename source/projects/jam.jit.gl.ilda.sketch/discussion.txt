<b>jam.jit.gl.ilda.sketch</b> uses a <o>jit.gl.sketch</o> object internally to render the graphics. Some of the jit.gl.sketch OB3D attrinutes are exposes through <b>jam.jit.gl.ilda.sketch.</b>. These are marked with <b>[OB3D]</b> in the description.<br/><br/>
<b>Opacity</b>
ILDA files do not contain opacitity values because they are commands for laser beam movement and colors. However, for screen rendering with <b>jam.jit.gl.ilda.sketch</b> you can apply opacities in two different ways:<ul><li>Globally to all lines by setting the <m>overrideopacity</m> attribute to 1 (enabled) and set the opacity with the <m>opacity</m>attribute</li><li>For rendering frames that use indexed colors: by setting the <m>custompalette</m> attribute to 1 (enabled) and defining custom pallet colors that have opacity values</li></ul>
